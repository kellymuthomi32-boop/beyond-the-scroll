About Beyond the Scroll

Meet the discovery brand exploring remarkable science, nature, animals, space and the questions that make the world worth understanding.

Curiosity is where discovery begins.

Beyond the Scroll is an educational discovery brand created to help people pause, look closer and explore the extraordinary details hidden in the natural world and the wider universe.

We share short-form videos and written discoveries about animals, biology, space, the deep ocean and surprising scientific ideas. Our goal is to make complex subjects approachable without losing the wonder that makes them worth learning about.

What you will find here

  • Remarkable nature: animal abilities, ecosystems and life in extreme environments.
  • Science made approachable: explanations that make fascinating ideas easier to understand.
  • Space and the universe: astronomical discoveries and the scale of the cosmos.
  • Curiosity-led stories: questions that invite further reading instead of asking readers to accept a claim without evidence.

Our principles

We value curiosity, clarity, responsible sourcing and the willingness to correct mistakes. A dramatic fact should still be treated carefully: context matters, scientific understanding can change, and a short video cannot always tell the whole story.

Read how we approach evidence on our Editorial Standards page, or send a specific correction through Contact Us.
