Editorial Standards & Fact-Checking

How Beyond the Scroll approaches research, sources, corrections and the difference between established findings and claims that need more context.

Our editorial promise

Beyond the Scroll aims to make science and nature engaging without presenting speculation as established fact. We seek reputable sources, preserve important context and welcome evidence-based corrections. These standards describe our intended approach; they are not a claim that every page has undergone independent scientific peer review.

Research and sources

Where practical, discovery articles include links to relevant scientific, educational or institutional sources. We favour primary research and reputable scientific institutions when suitable, and use established educational publishers to help explain complex subjects. A homepage card or short video may simplify a topic, so readers should consult the linked source for methods, limitations and full context.

Accuracy and uncertainty

  • We aim to distinguish observations, research findings, hypotheses and speculation.
  • We avoid implying that an animal or organism is invincible when research only shows survival under particular conditions.
  • We try to keep measurements and scientific terms in context rather than using a striking number without explanation.
  • We recognise that research may be updated, challenged or refined over time.

Images, media and attribution

Visuals may come from public-domain collections, Creative Commons licences or other credited sources. Where an image credit or licence is provided, follow the linked source and licence terms before reusing it. A credit does not automatically mean every use is permitted; users should verify the applicable licence and any conditions.

Corrections

If you believe something is inaccurate, contact us through our Contact Us page. Include the page or video title, the specific statement, and a reliable source that supports the correction. We review clear, evidence-based reports and may update wording or sources where appropriate. We cannot promise a particular response time or that every suggested change will be adopted.

Independence and endorsements

Do not assume that a linked institution, publisher or social platform endorses Beyond the Scroll. Links are provided for reference and further learning. If sponsored content or paid partnerships are introduced, they should be clearly identified in the relevant content.

Continuous improvement

These standards may evolve as the website grows. Our aim is to make curiosity the beginning of learning, not a substitute for evidence.
