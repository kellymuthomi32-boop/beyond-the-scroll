Privacy Notice

A clear explanation of what Beyond the Scroll stores in your browser and what may happen when you use embedded media or follow external links.

Last reviewed: 29 October 2026

1. The short version

Beyond the Scroll is a public discovery website. It does not currently offer user accounts, request profile details, or provide a first-party form for collecting messages. The website's Saved discoveries feature stores your selection in your browser using local storage.

2. Information stored on your device

If you save discoveries, your browser may store the saved item identifiers in local storage so the list remains available when you return using the same browser and device. This data is not an account and is not automatically sent to us. Clearing your browser's site data may remove it.

3. External services and embedded content

The site loads Google Fonts and displays embedded YouTube videos. Loading or interacting with third-party resources may allow those providers to receive technical information such as your IP address, browser details, and the page or video requested. Their handling of information is governed by their own policies and settings.

4. Links to other websites

When you follow a link to another website or social platform, you leave our site. We do not control the privacy practices, content or security of third-party services.

5. Children and families

This is a general-interest educational website. It is not designed to collect personal information from children, and no account registration is provided. Parents and guardians should supervise children's use of external platforms, which have their own age rules and privacy settings.

6. Security and changes

No website can promise absolute security. We aim to keep the site simple and avoid asking visitors for unnecessary personal information. This notice may be updated if the site's features or data practices change; the review date above indicates the latest stated review.

7. Contact

For privacy questions, use the official channels on our Contact Us page. Do not post private or sensitive information publicly in comments.
