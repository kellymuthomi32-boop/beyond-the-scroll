Help us respond clearly

  • For a correction, include the discovery or video title and the specific claim.
  • For a topic suggestion, tell us what question you want answered.
  • For collaboration enquiries, briefly describe your idea and the channel or project involved.

We do not publish a direct email address or an on-site contact form at this time. We will not pretend a form has been sent when no form-submission service is connected. Use the official profiles linked above.

Our commitment

We aim to treat questions respectfully, review corrections carefully and improve our content when reliable evidence shows that an update is needed. Please allow time for a response; replies are not guaranteed on every platform.
