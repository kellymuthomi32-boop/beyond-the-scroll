Cookie & Local Storage Notice

Learn how browser storage works on Beyond the Scroll and how third-party media may use technologies on their own services.

Last reviewed: 29 October 2026

1. Do we use cookies?

Beyond the Scroll does not currently set its own advertising or analytics cookies as part of the site's core features. The Saved discoveries feature uses browser local storage, which is different from a cookie, to remember saved items on the same browser and device.

2. What local storage does

Local storage allows your browser to remember the IDs of discoveries you save. It supports the saved-items feature and is not used by this site to identify you by name. If you clear site data, switch browsers, or use another device, your saved list may not be available there.

3. Third-party technologies

Google Fonts and embedded YouTube videos are supplied by third parties. Those services may use cookies or similar technologies, or receive technical information, according to their own settings and policies. Social links open external platforms whose practices we do not control.

4. Your choices

  • Use your browser settings to inspect, restrict or clear site data.
  • If you do not want to load an embedded video, use the direct YouTube link only when you choose to open it; note that the page currently loads the embedded video frame.
  • Review the relevant provider's privacy and cookie controls for choices available on that service.

5. Changes to this notice

If the site adds analytics, advertising, accounts, or other technologies that materially change its storage practices, this notice should be updated to describe them. This page is informational and is not a guarantee that third-party services never use cookies.

6. Questions

Contact us through our official contact channels.
